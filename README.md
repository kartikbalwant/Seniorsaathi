SeniorSaathi – Website Description

SeniorSaathi is a proposed elderly-care website designed to support senior citizens by making daily life safer, easier, and more comfortable. The website aims to connect elderly people with their family members and trusted volunteers who can help them with their daily needs.

The main features include daily check-ins, medicine reminders, family notifications, pension and government scheme assistance, and volunteer support. The website provides language options such as Hindi, Bhojpuri, and English to make it easier for users to understand and use the platform.

SeniorSaathi is designed with a simple, user-friendly interface and separate options for senior citizens, family members or caregivers, and volunteers. Its main objective is to reduce loneliness, improve communication, and help elderly people access support in their communities.
