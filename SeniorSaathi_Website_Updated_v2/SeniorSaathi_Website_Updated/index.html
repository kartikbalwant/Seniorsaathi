HOW SENIORSAATHI WORKS

A little support can make a big difference.

SeniorSaathi is a proposed service concept that connects a simple check-in with a clear human response.

1
☏

A simple check-in

The senior responds by voice, a simple tap, or a missed call in the planned service.

2
♧

A trusted person checks

If there is no response, the proposed workflow alerts an approved local volunteer to follow up.

3
⌂

Family can be informed

Family notifications happen according to the senior’s consent and configured preferences.
